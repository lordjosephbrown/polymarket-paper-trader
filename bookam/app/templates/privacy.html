Privacy Policy

In line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we collect

For businesses: your business name, phone number, locations, and services. For customers: your name, phone number, booking details, and — for home visits — the address you provide. We collect only what's needed to run your bookings.

How it's used

To create and manage appointments, process deposit payments (via Paystack — we never see or store your MoMo PIN or card details), send booking confirmations, reminders and status updates on WhatsApp, and show businesses their own booking history.

Business updates

Businesses may send occasional updates to their past customers. Reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time to opt out — booking messages still come through.

Sharing

Your details are shared only with the business you book with, and with our payment (Paystack) and messaging (Meta WhatsApp) providers as needed to deliver the service. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data or ask for it to be deleted by contacting the business you booked with or the Bookam operator. Businesses can export their own data anytime from the Reports page.
