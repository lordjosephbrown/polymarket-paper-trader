Terms of Service

The service

Bookam provides appointment booking, deposit collection, and customer messaging tools for service businesses in Ghana. Bookam is not a party to the service agreement between a business and its customer.

Deposits

Deposits lock a booking slot and are payable to the business. If a customer cancels or doesn't show, the deposit is kept by the business unless it chooses otherwise. If a business cancels, it must refund the customer's deposit.

Acceptable use

No spam: business updates are limited and customers can opt out at any time. Businesses must describe services and prices honestly, honour confirmed bookings, and use customer contact details only in connection with those bookings.

Payments

Payments are processed by Paystack. Bookam never stores MoMo PINs or full card details. Payment disputes are resolved between the customer, the business, and the payment provider.

Accounts

Businesses are responsible for keeping their password safe and for the accuracy of their listings. Accounts used fraudulently or abusively may be suspended.
